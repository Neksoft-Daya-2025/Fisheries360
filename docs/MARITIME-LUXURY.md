# Fisheries360 — Maritime luxury adaptation

The supplied Aventis prompt is used as a design reference for Fisheries360, not as a source of business facts.

Implemented:
- Obsidian navy #070F17, champagne #C59B5F, alabaster #FAF8F5 and parchment #F4EFEA.
- Editorial serif marketing headings with the existing self-hosted sans-serif for data interfaces. Georgia is used as a system serif: no remote font dependency.
- Full-width, naturally coloured aquaculture video, right-aligned desktop hero, three selectable story chapters and warm gold CTAs.
- Responsive editorial field introduction, two product showcases, native scroll-snap use-case gallery, refined hardware interactions and matching dashboard treatment.
- All six distinct pages, real saved-enquiry form and separate existing admin/backend retained.

Deliberate adaptations:
- The optimised MP4 replaces a 240-image canvas runway. There is no scroll hijacking, custom cursor, autoplay carousel or heavy mobile frame cache.
- Initial homepage headline is retained as requested; later chapters describe monitoring and operations.
- Product information remains grounded in the Fisheries360 brochure. No yacht products, invented customers, pricing, press endorsements, testimonials or production domain have been imported.
- Search/booking modals, newsletters and charter controls were omitted because they do not serve the authorised aquaculture product workflows. Request a Demo uses the existing working enquiry form.
- New editorial layout text and story chapters are code-managed; existing CMS content remains editable through its established page/section workflows.
